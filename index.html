About breast reduction

Millennial Plastic Surgery in Long Island offers consultations for breast reduction, also called reduction mammoplasty. Your surgeon can discuss breast tissue reduction, reshaping, contouring, and symmetry based on your individual needs and goals.

People explore breast reduction for different reasons. At your appointment, share the concerns that matter to you and ask what treatment may be appropriate. Your care team can explain the procedure, expected recovery, and follow-up care.

What to discuss at your consultation

Your goals and options

Discuss the changes you hope to make, your medical history, and the surgical approach your surgeon recommends. Ask about benefits, risks, and alternatives relevant to you.

Recovery and follow-up

Ask how to prepare, what recovery may involve, and how the practice supports post-surgery care. Your surgeon can give guidance tailored to your procedure and health.

Insurance questions for breast reduction

Some medically indicated breast reduction procedures may qualify for insurance coverage, depending on your plan and its criteria. Coverage is not guaranteed. Insurers may request records or other documentation before making a decision.

  • Ask your insurance company which requirements apply to your plan.
  • Ask the practice what records or documentation may be needed for review.
  • Confirm benefits, authorization, and any out-of-pocket costs directly with your insurer.

For information about insurance and surgery, visit the practice’s insurance information page. Contact the practice to confirm which guidance applies to breast reduction.
